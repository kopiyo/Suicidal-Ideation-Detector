Explain the vanishing gradient problem in neural networks. What causes this issue, and what techniques can be used to mitigate or overcome it?

Answer:
The vanishing gradient problem occurs when gradients become very small during backpropagation, especially in deep networks. This prevents earlier layers from learning effectively.

Causes:

Deep networks
Sigmoid/tanh activations (small derivatives)
Repeated multiplication of small gradients

Solutions:

ReLU / Leaky ReLU
Batch normalization
Residual connections (ResNet)
Proper weight initialization (He/Xavier)
LSTM/GRU (for sequences)
